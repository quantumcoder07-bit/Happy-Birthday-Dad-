04 / From the heart

A letter I'd rather write by hand.

Dear Dad,

I know I don't always say it, and sometimes I probably don't show it enough, but I notice more than you think.

I notice the sacrifices. The hard work. The little things you do without expecting anyone to clap for you.

And as I grow older, I'm beginning to understand that some of the strongest things a father gives his child are invisible — confidence, courage, values, and the feeling that someone has your back.

I hope I can take everything you've taught me and build something meaningful with it. Something that makes you smile and quietly think, "Yeah... that's my son."

Happy Birthday, Dad.
